Advances in Modern Complexity Theory (Complexity Theory Update Meeting), 21-23 Jan 2026

Venue:

The Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Chennai

Nature of the Program

This event is intended to serve as an update meeting, where Indian researchers gather and present recent advances in the field (not necessarily their own work, though that is not excluded), and where doctoral and postdoctoral researchers get an opportunity to network with their counterparts in other institutes/cities.

Link to previous edition Jan 2025

Target audience: researchers (at all levels - masters and PhD scholars, postdoctoral fellows, faculty) based in India and working/interested in the field of computational complexity.
